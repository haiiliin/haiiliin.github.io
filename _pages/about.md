I am Hailin Wang (汪海林), a passionate researcher and developer in the field of geotechnical engineering. My work focuses on the application of multiscale micromechanical methods to understand the behavior of geomaterials under various loading conditions. I have a strong interest in computational modeling, numerical simulations, and the integration of machine learning techniques into geotechnical analysis.
